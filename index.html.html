This Privacy Policy explains how Axiom Solutions Teknoloji Limited Şirketi ("Axiom Solutions", "we", "us" or "our") handles information in connection with the One Tap Baby mobile application for iOS and Android (the "App"). By downloading, installing or using the App, you acknowledge that you have read and understood this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the App.

1. Data Controller

For the purposes of the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"), the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and other applicable laws, the data controller is Axiom Solutions Teknoloji Limited Şirketi, Türkiye. You can contact us at halilcangurell@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • The App does not require an account or sign-in.
  • Information you enter into the App (such as feeding, sleep, diaper or other baby-care logs) is stored locally on your device.
  • We do not collect, receive, sell, rent or share this information, and we have no access to it.
  • We do not use advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs in the App.
  • The App is a convenience tool for parents and caregivers. It is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

When you use the App, you may enter information about your baby's routine, such as feeding times, sleep times, diaper changes, notes and similar records ("User Content"). User Content is stored only in the App's local storage on your device. It is not transmitted to our servers, and we do not have any means to view, access, recover or restore it.

You are solely responsible for the User Content you choose to enter, including any information relating to a child or any other person. Please only enter information you are entitled to record, and avoid entering information that you do not need to.

4. Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, location, contacts, photos, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, or usage or behavioral data through the App. We do not create user profiles and we do not engage in tracking across other companies' apps or websites.

5. Platform Providers and Third Parties

The App is distributed through the Apple App Store and Google Play. These platform providers, as well as your device manufacturer and operating system, may collect certain information (for example, download data, purchase records, diagnostic and crash data you have opted in to share with them, and device backups). Such collection is carried out by those companies under their own privacy policies, independently of us, and we are not responsible for their practices. We encourage you to review:

  • Apple Privacy Policy: apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google Privacy Policy: policies.google.com/privacy

Aggregated, anonymous statistics that Apple or Google may make available to developers (such as the number of downloads) do not allow us to identify you.

6. Device Backups and Data Loss

Depending on your device settings, User Content may be included in device backups made by iCloud, Google or other backup services. Those backups are controlled by you and the relevant provider, not by us.

Because User Content is stored only on your device, it may be permanently lost if you delete the App, reset or lose your device, or if the data becomes corrupted. We do not keep copies and cannot restore lost data. You are responsible for maintaining any backups you consider necessary.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

User Content remains on your device until you delete it within the App (where available) or uninstall the App. Uninstalling the App removes its local data from your device, subject to any device backups you have made.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by adults (parents and caregivers). It is not directed to children, and children should not use the App. Information about a baby or child is entered only by the adult user and stays on that user's device; we do not collect it. If you believe that personal information of a child has been provided to us in any way, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. Security

User Content benefits from the security features of your device and operating system. We recommend protecting your device with a passcode, biometrics and up-to-date software. However, no method of electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee the absolute security of information stored on your device.

10. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under the KVKK (including Article 11), the GDPR, the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA/CPRA) or similar laws, such as the right to learn whether your personal data is processed, to access, correct or delete it, to object to processing, and to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Authority – KVKK Kurumu). Because we do not collect or hold personal data through the App, in most cases there will be no data held by us to provide, correct or delete; you can manage or delete your User Content directly on your device. You may still contact us at any time with questions or requests.

We do not sell or share personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

11. Not Medical Advice

The App is designed solely to help you record and organize everyday baby-care information. It is not a medical device and is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, monitor or prevent any disease or condition. Any information, summaries, reminders or statistics shown in the App are for general informational purposes only and are not a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always seek the advice of a pediatrician or other qualified health professional with any questions about your child's health. Never disregard professional advice or delay seeking it because of something in the App. In an emergency, call your local emergency number (112 in Türkiye) immediately.
